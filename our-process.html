THE JOURNEY

Our 8-Stage Construction Process

Every home we build follows this proven workflow — ensuring quality, clarity, and confidence at every milestone.

Stage 01

Free Site Visit & Initial Consultation

Our senior civil engineer visits your plot, evaluates soil type, drainage, road access, and orientation. You get an honest initial discussion on construction feasibility, approximate costs, and realistic timelines — all at zero charge.

Duration: 1–2 Days
Site Evaluation Report Soil Suitability Notes Rough Estimate Sketch

Stage 02

Architectural Design & 3D Floor Plan

Our in-house architects design a fully customized floor plan aligned with your family's lifestyle, Vastu principles, and local municipal regulations. We provide a detailed 3D exterior elevation and interior layout walkthrough for your approval before any work starts.

Duration: 5–10 Days
3D Elevation Render 2D Floor Plan Vastu-Certified Layout
Stage 03

Detailed Estimate & Agreement Signing

A comprehensive itemized cost breakdown — covering every material, labour charge, and finishing — is presented for your review. After mutual agreement, a formal construction contract is signed with a defined payment schedule tied to build milestones, not arbitrary dates.

Duration: 2–3 Days
BOQ Document Milestone Schedule Signed Agreement

Step 04
Stage 04

Foundation & Structural Work

Site clearing, foundation digging, PCC bed preparation, column footing, and anti-termite treatment form the critical base. We use M20/M25 concrete mix with TMT steel bars as per structural engineer specifications. This is the most critical phase — done without shortcuts.

Duration: 3–5 Weeks
Foundation Certificate Column Layout Photo Anti-Termite Receipt
Stage 05

Brick Masonry & RCC Frame Construction

Only 100% kiln-burnt red soil bricks are used in walls. RCC beams, columns, and slab shuttering are executed floor by floor. Our masons are experienced in traditional brick bonding techniques that dramatically improve thermal insulation and wall strength over hollow/fly-ash alternatives.

Duration: 8–16 Weeks
Brick Quality Test Report Stage Completion Photos Concrete Mix Certificate
Step 05
Step 06
Stage 06

Plumbing, Electrical & Waterproofing

Concealed copper wiring with MCB protection, ISI-marked CPVC plumbing lines, and terrace/bathroom waterproofing using crystalline chemical compounds are installed during this phase. All electrical and plumbing work is executed before plastering to ensure clean, hidden routing.

Duration: 3–4 Weeks
Electrical Layout Drawing Plumbing Pressure Test Waterproofing Report
Stage 07

Plastering, Tiling & Teakwood Finishes

Double-coat cement plastering, granite/vitrified tile flooring, seasoned teak main doors, and window frames with mosquito mesh shutters bring the structure to life. Interior paint, ceiling false-work, modular kitchen, and wardrobe fitting complete the living space transformation.

Duration: 5–8 Weeks
Finish Material Selections Teak Grade Certificate Tile Layout Plan

Step 08
Stage 08 — Final

Quality Inspection & Key Handover

A thorough joint inspection walkthrough is conducted with the homeowner. Any finishing punch-list items are resolved within 7 days. The official 10-Year Structural Warranty Card, all completion certificates, and the NOC from our engineer are handed over along with your home keys.

Duration: 3–5 Days
10-Year Warranty Card Completion Certificate House Keys 🗝️
