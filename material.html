Home Materials

Only the Finest Materials.
Absolutely No Compromises.

Every brick, every beam, every tile is handpicked against strict quality standards. We use zero fly-ash, zero hollow blocks, and zero substandard steel.

WHY MATERIALS MATTER

The Difference Between a House That Lasts 30 Years and One That Lasts 300 Years

In our 25+ years, we've seen homes built on compromised materials crumble in under a decade. Fly-ash bricks absorb moisture. Hollow blocks crack under load. Low-grade steel rusts through RCC within 15 years.

That's why we made a non-negotiable commitment at our founding: only Grade-A, ISI-certified, tested materials will go into a home we build.

ISI Certified Only

Every material carries BIS/ISI certification — steel, cement, pipes, and electrical fittings.

Zero Fly-Ash Bricks

We strictly prohibit fly-ash or hollow blocks on all our construction sites — no exceptions.

Third-Party Lab Tests

Concrete cube tests and brick compression tests are conducted by independent certified labs.

First-Grade Seasoned Teakwood

We use Grade-A Indian teak (Tectona grandis) seasoned for a minimum of 5 years, reducing moisture content to below 12%. Natural silica and teak oil make it termite-resistant, dimensionally stable, and long-lasting without chemical treatment.

Species Tectona grandis (Teak)
